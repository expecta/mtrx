About MTRX

Matrix Gaming (MTRX) is an esports organization built on the belief that strategy meets skill. Originally established in 2022, Matrix Gaming competed in the Fortnite scene, signing notable talent including Ritual, Bhronos, BryceX, Shadow1x, Avivv, and Cooper.

Today, Matrix Gaming returns with a renewed vision — building a long-lasting organization grounded in community, integrity, and growth. Expanding across multiple platforms, fostering meaningful relationships, and creating a connected community that thrives in and out of the game.
